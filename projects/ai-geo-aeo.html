01 PROJECT OVERVIEW

Preparing content for the AI-powered search era.

Search behaviour is changing as users increasingly interact with AI-powered search and answer engines. Instead of clicking through multiple search results, users can receive direct answers, summaries, recommendations, and sources.

This created an opportunity to explore how B2B technology brands can structure their content so it is useful not only for traditional search engines, but also for AI-driven discovery.

02 THE CHALLENGE

Search visibility is no longer only about rankings.

01

AI Search Discovery

02

Content needs to clearly address specific questions and search intent rather than simply targeting keywords.

03

Brand Visibility

AI search introduces new considerations around brand mentions, citations, recommendations, and contextual visibility.

04

Trust & Authority

Helpful, accurate, well-structured content becomes increasingly important when users rely on AI systems for information.

06 OUTCOME

Building a forward-looking content direction.

The strategy established a content direction connecting traditional SEO with emerging AI search behaviour and B2B technology conversations.

Instead of treating GEO and AEO as replacements for SEO, the approach focuses on creating useful, authoritative, structured content that can serve users across multiple discovery environments.
