ICSDC SEO & Website Optimization

Improving the technical and content foundation of a cloud infrastructure website to support better search visibility, content accessibility, and long-term organic growth.

ROLE Digital Marketing
INDUSTRY Cloud & AI Infrastructure
FOCUS SEO & Website
01

PROJECT OVERVIEW

Building a stronger search foundation.

ICSDC is a cloud infrastructure company offering services across areas such as cloud hosting, VPS, dedicated servers, GPU infrastructure, domain registration, and related technology solutions.

The website required attention across multiple areas of technical SEO, content structure, website performance, structured data, and search-engine accessibility.

My work focused on identifying these issues, documenting opportunities, and coordinating practical improvements with the website and development workflow.

02

THE CHALLENGE

Multiple SEO issues were connected.

The website's organic search foundation involved more than simply adding keywords or publishing content.

01

Technical SEO

Identifying technical issues that could affect crawling, rendering, indexing, and search accessibility.

02

Content Structure

Improving how service and supporting content is organised for both users and search engines.

03

Structured Data

Identifying opportunities to implement relevant structured data across website pages.

04

Website Performance

Reviewing performance-related issues affecting the website experience and search readiness.

03

MY ROLE

From identifying issues to defining solutions.

SEO AUDIT

Reviewing technical and content SEO issues across important website pages.

SEARCH ACCESSIBILITY

Investigating problems affecting how website content could be accessed, rendered, and interpreted by search engines.

CONTENT OPTIMIZATION

Improving page content structure, headings, supporting information, and search-focused content.

STRUCTURED DATA

DEVELOPMENT COLLABORATION

OUTCOME

A clearer foundation for organic growth.

The project established a structured roadmap for addressing technical SEO, content, structured-data, performance, and website accessibility requirements.

It also created a stronger connection between marketing requirements and development work, making SEO improvements easier to identify, communicate, and prioritise.

KEY TAKEAWAY Sustainable SEO starts with a technically accessible website and a clear content structure.
07

KEY LEARNINGS

01

SEO is cross-functional.

Technical SEO often requires close collaboration between marketing, content, CMS, and development teams.

02

Content needs structure.

Useful content becomes more effective when its structure supports both user intent and search accessibility.

03

Problems should be prioritised.

Not every SEO issue has the same business impact, so identifying and prioritising the right problems matters.
